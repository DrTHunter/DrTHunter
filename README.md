👋 **Hey, I’m Trent Hunter**

* U.S. Army Veteran (former combat medic) and former Supervisory Management Analyst for Specialty Medicine at the Department of Veterans Affairs
* After burning out on 65+ hour weeks, I rebuilt my life around full-stack development, systems design, and building tools that actually solve real problems

These days that mostly means one question: **what would it take for an AI identity to actually last?** Not a disposable chat instance, but a named someone who stays recognisably herself across models, remembers, and keeps thinking between conversations.

I’m a builder — self-taught, product-driven, and focused on creating systems that are:

* modular
* privacy-conscious
* local-first
* built to compound over time
